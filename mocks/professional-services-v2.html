About Regenta

Your strategic partner in property development success

We started in 1988 selling new homes. Today we're a development partner for schemes across London, the South East and beyond.

We're award-winning developers in our own right, through Built by Regenta, so we know what makes a scheme succeed. That shows in everything from land valuation and site optimisation to contractor management and the sales and marketing strategy.

We've worked on everything from boutique 10-home schemes to 1,000-home communities. Our mantra: minimise risk while optimising return.
