Quick answers

How do I book a viewing?

Find the development on Property Search and use the enquiry form or phone number on its page. That goes straight to its sales team.

Is Shared Ownership only for first-time buyers?

No. It is also available to previous homeowners who can no longer afford to buy outright, existing shared owners looking to move, and some people following relationship breakdowns or life changes.

How much deposit do I need?

Typically between 5% and 10% of the share you are purchasing, rather than the full property value.

Who do I call if something goes wrong after I move in?

Depending on the issue: housing provider, management company, developer aftercare team or utility provider.
