Mobile nav: three options tap the menu in each phone

A · Tabs inside the menu recommended

The bar keeps just the logo, search and menu, so nothing gets pushed off. The full-screen menu opens on the side you are on, with a Homes / Services switch at the top to flip to the other list.

B · Smaller logo, switch stays in the bar

On phones the logo shrinks to the Regenta mark, which frees enough room for Services / Homes, search and menu. The menu lists just the side you are on, like now.

C · Search and switch both in the menu

The bar is just logo and menu. Inside: a search field at the top, the links for your side, and a card at the bottom (within thumb reach) to jump to the other side.
