Buying with us

From first viewing to your keys

Every purchase is a little different, but most follow these steps.

Draft copy, from the Shared Ownership FAQs. Elle to check.

  1. Find a home and book a viewing

    Browse the developments, then book a viewing through the development's page or by calling its sales team.

  2. Reserve

    A reservation fee secures the home while the legal work and your mortgage are arranged.

  3. Mortgage and solicitor

    You appoint a solicitor (a panel familiar with Shared Ownership may be available) and arrange your mortgage.

  4. Exchange

    Both sides become legally committed. This is typically four to eight weeks after reserving.

  5. Completion and keys

    Usually one to four weeks after exchange. Then the home is yours.

Who are Regenta?

We're a London new homes sales and marketing company, founded by Murray Smith in 1988. We sell homes for developers and housing associations, including Shared Ownership and private sale.

Murray grew up on a council estate in Camden, and that's where his belief in good homes for every kind of buyer comes from.

Are you the developer?

Mostly we sell on behalf of the developer or housing association that built the homes. Each development page tells you who that is.

Through Built by Regenta, we also build homes ourselves.

Built by Regenta

How do I book a viewing?

Find the development on Property Search and use the enquiry form or phone number on its page. That goes straight to its sales team.
